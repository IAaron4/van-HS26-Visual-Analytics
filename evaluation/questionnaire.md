## Part 2, the three that matter

These are about the algorithm rather than the interface, and they are the reason for running two
conditions. Only asked in the condition that has the model.

| | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. I could tell how much the prediction was to be trusted | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| B. I understood why the model expected what it did | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| C. I would act on this prediction in a real job | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |

**A and C together are the interesting pair.** High C with low A is the bad case: willing to act
without being able to judge reliability. That is the pattern the design is supposed to prevent.
