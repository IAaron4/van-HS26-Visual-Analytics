# Consent to take part

**Study:** judging train disruptions with a predictive tool
**Module:** Visual Analytics (van), FHNW, HS26
**Run by:** Aaron Studer

## What this is

I have built a tool that finds disruptions in Swiss train data and predicts how a delay will
carry along a route. I want to find out whether it is usable and whether the prediction actually
helps, so I am asking a few people to try it while thinking aloud.

It takes about 45 minutes.

## What you will do

You will use two versions of the tool and answer three short questions in each, saying out loud
what you are looking at and why. Afterwards there is a short questionnaire.

**I am testing the tool, not you.** There is no score and no wrong answer. If something is
confusing, that is a result I need, so please say so.

## What is recorded

- The screen while you work
- Audio of what you say
- Your answers and how long each took
- The questionnaire

## What happens to it

- Recordings and notes are anonymised before I analyse anything. You appear as P1 to P8.
- Nothing identifiable goes into my repository or my report.
- Quotes may appear in the report, anonymised.
- Recordings are deleted once the module is marked, by the end of February 2027.
- Nothing is uploaded to any external AI service. FHNW data protection rules apply.

## Your choice

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any point, take a break, skip a question, or ask me to
delete your recording afterwards, and you do not have to give a reason. Nothing follows from
stopping.

## Questions

Ask me anything before we start, or afterwards.
